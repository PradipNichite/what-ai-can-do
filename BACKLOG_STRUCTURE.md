# Backlog Structure

This backlog has two content levels. Both can become publishable assets.

## 1. Umbrella Topic

An umbrella topic gives the learner a broad map.

Examples:

- Student AI Learning
- Applied Mathematics In AI
- Career Discovery After 12th
- AI-Era Careers And Work

An umbrella topic should not be only a category label. It should include its own possible visual story, usually 6 to 8 scenes, so it can become a mobile post, carousel, short video, or presentation.

## 2. Granular Episode

A granular episode goes deeper into one idea from the umbrella topic.

Examples:

- Derivatives: How AI Learns From Mistakes
- Matrices: How Images Become Numbers
- Biology But Not Doctor
- Careers That Did Not Look Serious 10 Years Ago

Granular episodes should have enough detail to become a full module, prompt archive, mobile story, Marathi version, or video storyboard.

## How To Decide The Level

Use an umbrella topic when the learner needs a map.

Use a granular episode when the learner needs one clear concept, example, workflow, or story.

Some ideas can exist at both levels. For example:

- Umbrella: How Students Can Use AI To Study Better
- Granular: How To Ask AI Better Study Questions
- Granular: Use AI With Textbook Photos
- Granular: How Not To Become Dependent On AI

## Required Sections For Umbrella Topics

- Core Promise
- Audience
- Why This Topic Matters
- Umbrella Post Treatment
- 9:16 Scene Flow
- What The Umbrella Post Should Teach
- Where Granular Episodes Go Deeper
- Granular Episode Ideas
- Best Formats
- Marathi / Local Notes
- Next Step

## Required Sections For Granular Episodes

- Core Hook
- Target Learner
- Why It Matters
- Possible 9:16 Visual Story
- Renderer Possibilities
- Marathi / Localization Notes
- Next Step

## Guiding Principle

Do not dump ideas into a list. Arrange them so the learner can move from curiosity to map to deeper understanding.
