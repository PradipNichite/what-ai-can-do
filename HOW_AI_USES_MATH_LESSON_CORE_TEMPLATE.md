## 7. Renderer-Agnostic Scene Flow

This is not yet a video script. It is the shared concept sequence.

Self-contained opening requirement:

The first scene/card/slide must stand on its own. It must name the lesson or topic, state what the learner will understand, connect the school concept to the AI use, and introduce the concrete example or task used by the rest of the lesson.

Native image-story composition requirement:

For generated image-only cards, text must be created as part of the image generation itself, not added as a separate overlay after the fact. Use poster typography, tablet UI, notebook panels, speech bubbles, sticky notes, diagram labels, arrows, and callouts that belong to the scene.
