# Research Guide

## Research Standard

Use current sources wherever possible. Do not rely only on memory for current product capabilities, limits, or examples.

Prefer:

- Official product documentation.
- Official product pages.
- Company blogs where the company is describing its own feature.
- Serious research or industry reports for broad claims.
- Indian public or business sources where Indian context matters.

Use YouTube and Reddit mainly for understanding public perception or examples, not as the main factual source.

## What to Research for Each Module

- Real examples of tools or companies doing this.
- Common user questions.
- Indian examples where possible.
- Simple analogies.
- Current capabilities.
- Limitations and safety concerns.
- Existing diagrams or explanations that can inspire structure.

## Source Notes

For every module, include at least 3 source links or research notes. Mark uncertain claims as uncertain.

Do not invent examples and present them as real.
