## What The Comparison Shows

There are now two visual directions in the math series.

## Direction A: Earlier Illustrated Style

Examples:

- Matrices
- Vectors v3
- Probability, partly

Strengths:

- softer, more illustrated
- closer to the original matrices/vectors look
- feels less like a realistic photo render

Risks:

- some earlier vector/probability attempts were less technically explicit
- character/style can drift between lessons
- abstract mechanisms may become icons and glows unless prompts are very strict

## Direction B: New Realistic Technical Study-Desk Style

Examples:

- Functions
- Series Intro
- Statistics
- Linear Equations

Strengths:

- highly readable technical overlays
- strong mechanism visibility
- internally consistent across the new batch
- good for high-school applied math because numbers, graphs, bars, and equations are clear

Risks:

- more realistic than the earlier established illustration style
- may feel like a new visual language unless we intentionally adopt it for this series
- character consistency still needs checking, but the environment is consistent

## Recommendation

Adopt Direction B for the dedicated "How AI Uses Math" series.

Reason:

This series is technical and applied. The new style is not as soft/illustrated, but it has stronger teaching clarity. The overlays are readable, the mechanisms are visible, and the contact sheets look coherent across Functions, Intro, Statistics, and Linear Equations.

Keep Matrices, Vectors, and Probability as existing drafts, but when finalizing the full series, consider refreshing them into Direction B if visual consistency matters more than preserving the earlier look.

## Production Rule Going Forward

For new math-series images:

- use the realistic warm study-desk/tablet technical style
- prioritize readable mechanisms over decorative illustration
- keep the same learner, teal/green shirt, wooden desk, lamp, notebook, books
- use tablet overlays with large numbers, graphs, vectors, matrices, score bars, and arrows
- avoid vague glowing AI visuals
