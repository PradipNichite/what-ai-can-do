# Customer Support Mobile Visual Story Prompts

Format: 9:16 mobile visual story for WhatsApp Status, YouTube Community, Shorts-style slides, or classroom mobile viewing.

Constraint: the story should be understandable by swiping through images. The images intentionally avoid explanatory text.

## Story Beats

1. Late order worry.
2. Human support checks a system.
3. AI without access cannot help properly.
4. AI gets secure access/tools.
5. AI gives a specific useful update.
6. Complex cases go to a human.
7. Final summary: customer + AI + tools + human + secure system.

## Card 1 Prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: 9:16 mobile visual story card 1 of 7
Primary request: Create a vertical mobile-first storytelling image with NO written text. Scene: an Indian teenager at a study desk looks worried while checking a phone showing a late delivery chat. A small shopping receipt and unopened notebook are on the desk. The image should clearly communicate: "My order is late, I need help."
Audience: Indian high school students, parents, and ordinary mobile viewers.
Style/medium: warm modern digital illustration, cinematic but simple, expressive faces, mobile story aesthetic, not corporate stock, not childish.
Composition/framing: 9:16 portrait, close-to-medium shot, phone visible, strong emotional hook, clean background, no panels.
Visual continuity: use this same teenager/customer style across the full story: Indian teenage boy, short black hair, blue casual T-shirt, study desk.
Constraints: absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no readable UI words, no logos, no trademarks, no watermark; communicate using expression, objects, icons, and body language only.
```
