# Customer Support Visual Journey Prompts

These prompts generated the five-image sequence used in `modules/business/ai-customer-support-agent.md`.

## Card 1

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: visual journey card 1 for an educational Markdown module
Primary request: Create the opening scene for a visual lesson titled "Where is my order?" showing an Indian teenager looking at a phone with a delayed order message.
Audience: Indian high school students and ordinary first-time AI learners.
Scene/backdrop: simple Indian home study corner, notebook on table, delivery receipt nearby, phone chat visible.
Subject: one student/customer holding a phone, curious and slightly worried, not dramatic.
Style/medium: clean modern educational illustration, warm and realistic, not corporate stock, not childish.
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape card, large readable phone/chat area, plenty of clean whitespace.
Text (verbatim): "Where is my order?"
Optional tiny phone message text, verbatim: "My order is late."
Constraints: render the text exactly; no logos or trademarks; no watermark; no dense infographic; one clear idea only; avoid extra labels.
```
