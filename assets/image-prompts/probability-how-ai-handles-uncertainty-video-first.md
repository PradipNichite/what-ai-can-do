# Probability: How AI Handles Uncertainty - Video-First Prompt Pack

These prompts are for source frames intended to become image-to-video clips. The goal is not just attractive images; each frame must visibly teach one technical step.

## Character / Style Bible

Recurring learner:

- Indian 11th/12th standard student
- dark wavy hair
- teal/green shirt
- curious, thoughtful, expressive face
- warm wooden study desk with notebook, pen, books, small plant
- cozy study-room background with warm desk lamp
- tablet or phone as the main concept surface
- warm modern Indian educational illustration, polished cinematic 9:16 mobile frame

Visual rule:

- Match the matrices and vectors-v3 visual language.
- Use the same warm student/tablet environment.
- Do not create isolated flat diagram cards.
- Use technical overlays on the tablet: probability bars, confidence meters, scales, option cards, and highlight states.
- Keep embedded text minimal but allow large readable numbers where they are the technical content.

## Technical Sequence

```text
input/question -> possible answers -> probability scale -> probability bars -> highest score -> uncertainty when scores are close -> real AI uses -> quick check
```

## Frame Teaching Table

| # | Frame | Learning job | Visible evidence | Motion target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Learning promise | AI estimates likely answers | tablet shows one input with multiple possible answers | option cards appear around input |
| 2 | Probability scale | probability is 0 to 1 / 0% to 100% | scale with 0, 0.5, 1 and confidence pointer | pointer slides along scale |
| 3 | Option scores | AI gives scores to choices | cat/dog/car bars and scores | bars grow to different heights |
| 4 | Highest score | AI picks the largest probability | top bar glows and selected answer card appears | largest bar highlights |
| 5 | Uncertainty | close scores mean weaker confidence | close cat/dog bars and low confidence meter | two bars pulse, meter drops |
| 6 | Real uses | same idea powers multiple AI systems | photo classifier, recommendation, next-word mini panels | mini panels highlight in sequence |
| 7 | Recap | confidence-weighted guess pipeline | input -> probabilities -> chosen answer chain | nodes light up left to right |
| 8 | Quick check | viewer compares clear vs uncertain predictions | two score sets side by side | uncertain set glows |
