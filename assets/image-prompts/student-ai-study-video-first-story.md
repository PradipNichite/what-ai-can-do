# Student AI Study Video-First Source Frame Prompts

These prompts are for source images intended to become image-to-video clips. They are intentionally less text-heavy than carousel cards.

Global style:

- Warm modern Indian educational illustration.
- Same Indian school student across all frames: teenage girl, green school T-shirt, black hair tied back, curious expression.
- Clean home study desk with textbook, notebook, pencil, and smartphone.
- Vertical 9:16 composition.
- Minimal text, large action area, clear motion potential.
- No watermark, no logos.

## Frame 1 - Stuck

Text: `Stuck?`

Student looks confused at an open science textbook. Phone nearby has a soft AI glow. Large clear character and textbook, minimal labels.

## Frame 2 - Photo

Text: `Show the page`

Student lifts phone camera over textbook page. The page is clearly visible. Motion implied by hand and phone position.

## Frame 3 - Context

Text: `Class + Topic + Doubt`

Student points at textbook while phone assistant receives three simple context bubbles. Keep text large and minimal.

## Frame 4 - Steps

Text: `Simple steps`

Phone shows three clean step cards. Student has an aha expression. Textbook diagram glows softly.

## Frame 5 - Practice

Text: `Practice, don't copy`

Student writes in notebook. Phone shows a hint card, not the final answer.

## Frame 6 - Feedback

Text: `Check my answer`

Notebook answer gets a checkmark and small correction bubble from AI. Student compares phone and notebook.

## Frame 7 - Verify

Text: `AI helps. You practice. Teacher verifies.`

Final balanced setup with student, phone AI, textbook, and teacher/parent verification.
