# Vectors: How AI Compares Meaning - Video-First Prompt Pack v2

This v2 prompt pack corrects the visual direction after v1 drifted into flat diagram cards. Match the matrices lesson style: warm Indian student study setting, cinematic educational illustration, tablet-based concept overlays, and minimal embedded text.

## Character / Style Bible

Recurring learner:

- Indian 11th/12th standard student
- dark wavy hair
- teal shirt
- curious, calm, thoughtful expression
- seated at a wooden study desk with notebook, pen, small plant, desk lamp
- cozy room background with books/posters
- warm modern Indian educational illustration
- polished, mobile-first, 9:16 vertical

Visual rule:

- Use the same student/tablet world as the matrices lesson.
- Avoid flat white poster cards.
- Avoid dense labels and equations.
- Prefer icons, dots, arrows, glowing overlays, and tablet UI.
- Keep source frames understandable when paused, but let voiceover explain the technical detail.
- Leave safe space for sparse captions.
