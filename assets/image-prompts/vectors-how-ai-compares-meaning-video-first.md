# Vectors: How AI Compares Meaning - Video-First Prompt Pack

These prompts are for source frames intended to become image-to-video clips. Keep embedded text minimal because the voiceover carries the explanation.

## Character / Style Bible

Recurring learner:

- Indian 11th/12th standard student
- curious, calm, thoughtful
- dark hair, simple teal school/casual outfit
- clean study desk or simplified educational diagram space
- warm modern Indian educational illustration
- polished, mobile-first, 9:16 vertical

Visual rule:

- One visual action per frame.
- Minimal embedded text.
- Large vectors, points, and arrows.
- Clear foreground/background separation.
- Avoid dense equations.
- Keep enough margin for subtle camera movement.
