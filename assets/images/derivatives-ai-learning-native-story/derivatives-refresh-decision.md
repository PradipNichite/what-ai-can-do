However, it was made before the current series rules were clarified:

- image-first review before video generation
- avoid poster-heavy cards for video

The current derivative frames are closer to integrated story/poster cards than video-first source frames. They can work, but they do not match the visual language now being used for Functions, Statistics, and Linear Equations.

## What To Keep

- memory anchor: `Derivative tells AI which way to adjust.`
- derivative as local slope
- error as a function of model weight
- positive slope means one direction increases error
- update should move opposite the slope
- repeated small corrections reduce error
