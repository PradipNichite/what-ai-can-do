# After 12th Science: What Are The Real Options?

Status: Backlog idea.

Parent topic: [Career Discovery After 12th](../topics/career-discovery-after-12th.md)

## Core Hook

A student finishes 12th Science and feels there are only two respectable paths: doctor or engineer. Then the camera zooms out and many more doors appear.

## Target Learner

11th/12th students, parents, and career counselors.

## Why It Matters

Many students make career decisions with a very narrow map.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Student sees only two doors: Doctor and Engineer.
2. Camera zooms out to many more doors.
3. Doors are grouped by interests: biology, computers, design, research, business, environment.
4. Each path shows one real work scene.
5. End: choose by work you want to do, not only degree name.

## Candidate Branches

- Engineering
- Medical and allied health
- Pure sciences
- Biotechnology
- Bioinformatics
- Food Technology
- Data Science
- Design
- Psychology
- Pharmacy
- Agriculture and environmental science
- Forensics
- Research and academia
