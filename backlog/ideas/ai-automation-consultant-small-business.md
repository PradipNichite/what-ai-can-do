# AI Automation Consultant For Small Businesses

Status: Backlog idea.

Parent topic: [AI-Era Careers And Work](../topics/ai-era-careers-and-work.md)

## Core Hook

Small businesses repeat many tasks: replies, invoices, summaries, reports, follow-ups. AI can help automate parts of that workflow.

## Target Learner

College students, working professionals, and small business owners.

## Why It Matters

This turns AI from a chat toy into practical operational help.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Shop owner gets repeated customer messages.
2. Consultant maps the workflow.
3. AI drafts replies and checks data when connected to tools.
4. Human handles exceptions.
5. Business saves time without losing control.

## Renderer Ideas

- Small business workflow visual story
- Service package examples
- Marathi explainer for local businesses

## Next Step

Use WhatsApp orders, appointment booking, or invoice summaries as the first example.
