# AI-Era Careers Overview

Status: Backlog idea.

Parent topic: [AI-Era Careers And Work](../topics/ai-era-careers-and-work.md)

## Core Hook

Earlier, career often meant degree -> job. Now people can build income through skills, tools, content, services, and AI-assisted workflows.

## Target Learner

High school students, college students, young professionals, and parents.

## Why It Matters

Many new opportunities are not explained well in traditional career guidance.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Student sees the old career ladder.
2. New map appears: skills, tools, content, services, automation, community.
3. AI helps with drafts, visuals, research, coding, translation, and operations.
4. Human judgment decides quality and trust.
5. End: new careers are built by combining domain skill with AI tools.
