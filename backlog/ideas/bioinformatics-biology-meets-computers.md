# Bioinformatics: When Biology Meets Computers

Status: Backlog idea.

Parent topic: [Career Discovery After 12th](../topics/career-discovery-after-12th.md)

## Core Hook

Biology now creates huge data: DNA sequences, protein data, disease records, and lab results. Bioinformatics uses computers to find patterns in that data.

## Target Learner

11th/12th Biology students and students interested in both biology and computers.

## Why It Matters

It shows a path for Biology students who also like data, coding, or AI.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Biology student sees DNA as a long code.
2. Lab produces too much data to inspect manually.
3. Computer tools compare sequences.
4. Patterns help researchers understand disease or traits.
5. End: Bioinformatics is biology plus data thinking.

## Technical Point To Touch

Biological information can be represented as data. Algorithms compare, search, align, and find patterns in that data.
