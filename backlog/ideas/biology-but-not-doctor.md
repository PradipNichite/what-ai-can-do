# Biology But Not Doctor: What Else Can I Do?

Status: Backlog idea.

## Core Hook

Many Biology students think not getting MBBS means failure. Show meaningful alternatives where biology is still useful.

## Target Learner

11th/12th Biology students and parents.

## Why It Matters

This can reduce panic and open a more realistic career map.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Student sees MBBS as the only visible path.
2. The path feels blocked.
3. More paths appear: Bioinformatics, Biotechnology, Food Technology, Pharmacy, Genetics, Public Health.
4. Each path shows a real work scene.
5. End: Biology can lead to many kinds of work.

## Episode Branches

- Bioinformatics
- Biotechnology
- Food Technology
- Pharmacy and clinical research
- Genetics and genomics
- Public health

## Renderer Ideas

- 9:16 career story
- Marathi version for parents
- Printable one-page career map
