# Biotechnology: Using Living Systems To Build Useful Things

Status: Backlog idea.

## Core Hook

Biotechnology uses living systems, cells, microbes, enzymes, or biological processes to solve real problems.

## Target Learner

11th/12th Biology students and parents.

## Why It Matters

It makes biology feel applied, not only memorization.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Student thinks biology means only diagrams and definitions.
2. Lab scene shows microbes/cells doing useful work.
3. Output connects to vaccines, agriculture, diagnostics, enzymes, or fermentation.
4. Student sees biology becoming a product/process.
5. End: Biotechnology turns life science into real-world solutions.

## Technical Point To Touch

Living systems can be studied, modified, or used to produce useful materials, tests, or processes.

## Renderer Ideas

- Lab-to-real-world visual story
- Comparison with bioinformatics and food technology
- Career map card

## Next Step

Choose one case: vaccine, enzyme, crop improvement, or diagnostic test.
