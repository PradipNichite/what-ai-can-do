# Data Storyteller

Status: Backlog idea.

Parent topic: [AI-Era Careers And Work](../topics/ai-era-careers-and-work.md)

## Core Hook

Many people have spreadsheets and reports, but they do not know what the data is saying. A data storyteller turns numbers into decisions.

## Target Learner

College students, office workers, teachers, small business owners.

## Why It Matters

AI can help summarize and visualize data, but the human must ask the right question and interpret the result.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Person opens a confusing spreadsheet.
2. AI helps find trends and outliers.
3. Chart turns numbers into a story.
4. Human decides what action to take.
5. End: data is useful only when it changes a decision.

## Renderer Ideas

- Spreadsheet-to-story visual
- Interactive mini dashboard
- Printable data-question checklist

## Next Step

Pick one dataset: shop sales, student marks, survey feedback, or expenses.
