# Derivatives: How AI Learns From Mistakes

Status: Prototype created.

## Core Hook

Students learn derivatives in 11th/12th standard, but often do not see why they matter. Show how slope, error, and small updates help AI improve.

## Target Learner

11th/12th science students and first-year engineering/data-science learners.

## Why It Matters

This connects school calculus to AI training without starting from formal rules.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. AI makes a wrong prediction.
2. Derivative at a point shows tangent slope.
3. Slope becomes a direction clue.
4. AI measures prediction error.
5. Error curve shows which way to move.
6. The math glimpse shows `slope = Delta error / Delta weight`.
7. AI repeats small corrections.
8. Derivatives feel useful in the real world.

## Technical Point To Touch

Derivative is local rate of change. In AI training, the useful glimpse is: if changing a weight slightly increases error, move the weight in the opposite direction by a small step.
