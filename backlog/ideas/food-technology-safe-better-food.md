# Food Technology: Science Behind Safe And Better Food

Status: Backlog idea.

## Core Hook

Food Technology uses science to make food safe, nutritious, tasty, scalable, and shelf-stable.

## Target Learner

11th/12th Science students and parents.

## Why It Matters

It connects chemistry, biology, engineering, nutrition, and industry to everyday food.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Student opens a packet of milk/snacks/grains.
2. Behind the packet is testing, processing, packaging, and safety.
3. Lab checks quality and shelf life.
4. Factory scales production.
5. End: Food Technology is science people eat every day.

## Technical Point To Touch

Food safety and quality depend on testing, preservation, processing, packaging, and standards.
