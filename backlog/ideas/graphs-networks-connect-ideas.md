# Graphs: How Networks Connect Ideas

Status: Backlog idea.

## Core Hook

Many real systems are networks: friends, roads, websites, recommendations, and knowledge.

## Target Learner

High school students and college students.

## Why It Matters

Graphs make recommendations, routes, search, and social networks easier to understand.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Student sees a map of friends or cities.
2. Each person/place is a node.
3. Each relationship/road is an edge.
4. AI uses the network to suggest a friend, route, video, or product.
5. End: graphs are math for connected things.

## Technical Point To Touch

A graph has nodes and edges. Algorithms can search paths, find clusters, and recommend related nodes.

- Network visual story
- Interactive click-to-find-path demo
- Career link: maps, social media, recommendation systems
