# Local-Language AI Educator

Status: Backlog idea.

Parent topic: [AI-Era Careers And Work](../topics/ai-era-careers-and-work.md)

## Core Hook

Many people understand technology better when it is explained in their own language and context.

## Target Learner

Students, parents, teachers, small business owners, and local-language creators.

## Why It Matters

AI education should not feel like imported English jargon. Natural Marathi/Hindi/local-language explanation can make technology accessible.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Person hears a technical AI term and feels lost.
2. Educator explains it in natural Marathi-English.
3. Familiar examples make it click.
4. Learner tries one useful prompt.
5. End: local language is not a weakness; it is access.

## Renderer Ideas

- Marathi mobile story
- Short video lesson
- Glossary of technical words to keep in English

Build from the Marathi localization guide and make one pilot episode.
