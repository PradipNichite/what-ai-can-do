# Matrices: How Images Become Numbers

Status: video-interactive
Pipeline stage: 08 Video / Interactive

Parent topic: [Applied Mathematics In AI](../topics/applied-mathematics-ai.md)

## Core Hook

A photo looks visual to us, but to a computer it can become a grid of numbers.

## Target Learner

11th/12th science students and students curious about AI/computer vision.

## Why It Matters

Matrices often feel abstract. Image pixels make the idea concrete.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Student takes a selfie or photo of a dog.
2. The image zooms into pixels.
3. Each pixel becomes numbers for color/brightness.
4. The whole image becomes a matrix/grid.
5. AI uses those numbers to detect patterns.
6. End: matrices are not only exam tables; they help computers see.

## Technical Point To Touch

A grayscale image can be represented as a matrix where each cell stores brightness. A color image can use multiple matrices/channels.
