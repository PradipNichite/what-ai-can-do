# No-Code Builder

Status: Backlog idea.

## Core Hook

You can build useful websites, forms, dashboards, and automations without becoming a full software engineer.

## Target Learner

College students, small business owners, teachers, and professionals.

## Why It Matters

No-code plus AI can help people solve local problems quickly.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Small team tracks work in messy WhatsApp messages and spreadsheets.
2. No-code tool creates a simple form and dashboard.
3. AI helps plan fields, logic, and messages.
4. Work becomes easier to track.
5. End: building small tools is now more accessible.
