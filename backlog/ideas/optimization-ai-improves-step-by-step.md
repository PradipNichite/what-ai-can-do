# Optimization: How AI Improves Step By Step

Status: Backlog idea.

Parent topic: [Applied Mathematics In AI](../topics/applied-mathematics-ai.md)

## Core Hook

AI improves by finding settings that reduce error, step by step.

## Target Learner

11th/12th science students and early college learners.

## Why It Matters

Optimization connects derivatives, error, training, and real AI learning into one story.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. AI starts on a hill with high error.
2. It checks which direction goes down.
3. It takes a small step.
4. It checks error again.
5. Many steps bring it near the lowest point.

## Technical Point To Touch

Optimization is choosing settings that minimize error/loss. A derivative or gradient can guide the next step.

## Renderer Ideas

- Valley/hill visual story
- Interactive slider where step size changes learning
- Video animation showing the point moving downhill
