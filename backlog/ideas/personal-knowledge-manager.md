# Personal Knowledge Manager

Status: Backlog idea.

Parent topic: [AI-Era Careers And Work](../topics/ai-era-careers-and-work.md)

## Core Hook

People have notes, PDFs, screenshots, bookmarks, and documents everywhere. AI can help organize and retrieve what matters.

## Target Learner

Students, researchers, professionals, and creators.

## Why It Matters

Knowledge work is not only creating content; it is also finding, connecting, and using past material.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Person has scattered notes and files.
2. AI helps summarize and tag them.
3. Related ideas become connected.
4. Person asks a question and finds the right note.
5. End: memory becomes searchable.

- Personal workflow checklist
