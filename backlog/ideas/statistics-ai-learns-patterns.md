# Statistics: How AI Learns Patterns From Many Examples

Status: Backlog idea.

## Core Hook

One example is not enough. AI learns patterns from many examples.

## Target Learner

High school and college students.

## Why It Matters

Statistics explains why data size, bias, averages, outliers, and patterns matter in AI.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. One student's marks do not show a full pattern.
2. Many students' marks reveal trends.
3. Outliers appear.
4. AI learns from the pattern, not just one case.
5. Bad or biased data gives bad lessons.

## Technical Point To Touch

Statistics studies patterns in data. AI systems often learn from distributions, averages, variation, and repeated examples.

## Renderer Ideas

- Classroom marks visual story
- Simple chart-based mobile story
- Printable activity with small dataset

## Next Step

Choose a student-friendly dataset: marks, sports scores, or weather.
