# Vectors: How AI Compares Meaning

Status: Backlog idea.

## Core Hook

AI can compare meanings by placing words, images, or users as points/directions in a number space.

## Target Learner

11th/12th students, college students, and anyone curious about recommendation/search systems.

## Why It Matters

Vectors explain why AI search can find related ideas even when exact words do not match.

## Possible 9:16 Visual Story

1. Student searches "medicine for fever".
2. AI also understands related words like doctor, clinic, temperature, tablet.
3. Words appear as points on a meaning map.
4. Similar meanings are closer together.
5. AI uses distance/similarity to retrieve useful results.

## Technical Point To Touch

A vector is a list of numbers with direction and length. Similarity can be understood as closeness or angle between vectors.
