# AI-Era Careers And Work

Status: High-level topic track.

## Core Promise

Show how generative AI, creator economy, automation, no-code tools, and gig work are changing what a career can look like.

## Audience

High-school students, college students, early professionals, freelancers, parents, and small business owners.

## Why This Topic Matters

Many new opportunities do not look like traditional careers. Students need examples that show the work, skills, tools, and judgment involved, instead of only hearing vague advice like "learn AI."
