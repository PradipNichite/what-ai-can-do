# Applied Mathematics In AI

Status: High-level topic track.

## Core Promise

Make students feel that mathematics is not useless by showing how school math quietly powers AI, engineering, data science, search, maps, recommendations, and image systems.

## Audience

11th/12th science students, first-year engineering students, curious high-school students, teachers, and parents.

## Why This Topic Matters

Many students learn derivatives, matrices, probability, vectors, functions, statistics, and graphs as exam topics. They often see formulas before purpose. This track should reverse that: start from a real system, then reveal the math idea inside it.

## Design Principle

Do not stop at analogy. Each episode should include at least one frame that touches the actual mathematical idea in a simple way, such as slope, rate of change, grid of numbers, vector distance, probability score, or error reduction.
