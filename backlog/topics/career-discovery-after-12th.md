# Career Discovery After 12th

Status: High-level topic track.

## Core Promise

Help students and parents see career options beyond the two default answers: doctor or engineer.

## Audience

10th to 12th standard students, parents, teachers, career counselors, and students who are confused after board exams or entrance results.

## Why This Topic Matters

Career choices often feel binary and high-pressure. Many good fields remain invisible because students do not see what the work actually looks like. This track should explain careers through real-life use cases, not just lists of degree names.
