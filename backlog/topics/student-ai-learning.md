# Student AI Learning

Status: High-level topic track.

## Core Promise

Show students how AI can help them study better without turning them into copy-paste learners.

## Audience

8th to 12th standard students, parents, teachers, and coaching-class learners.

## Why This Topic Matters

This is one of the most relatable entry points for AI. A student does not need to understand business automation or model architecture. They already know the feeling of being stuck on homework, textbook language, exam pressure, or a hard concept.

## Marathi / Local Notes

Keep the tone very natural. Students may say AI, photo, question, answer, textbook, exam, practice, and test in English while speaking Marathi. Do not over-translate those words.
