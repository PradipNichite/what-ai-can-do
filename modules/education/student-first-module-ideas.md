# Student-First Module Ideas

The customer support example is useful for showing business automation, but it is not the most natural first hook for 8th standard or high school students.

For students, the first examples should start from situations they already care about.

## Recommended Next Example

### AI Explains A Textbook Page

Hook:

A student is stuck on a science textbook page. The page has a diagram, hard English, and a few confusing terms. The student takes a photo and asks AI to explain it simply.

Why this is stronger:

- Every student has experienced textbook confusion.
- It naturally shows multimodal AI: image + text + explanation + questions.
- It can become a visual story easily.
- It is useful for students, parents, and teachers.
- It can be localized into Marathi naturally.

Possible 9:16 mobile story:

1. Student sees a confusing textbook page.
2. Student feels stuck but does not want to ask in class.
3. Student takes a photo.
4. AI reads the page and diagram.
5. AI explains it in simple language.
6. AI gives a real-life example.
7. AI asks practice questions.
8. Student answers.
9. AI gives hints, not just final answers.

Core idea:

> AI can become a personal study helper when the student shares the right page, asks the right question, and checks the answer with learning material or a teacher.

## Other Strong Student Hooks

### AI Turns A Hard English Paragraph Into Simple Marathi

Good for students with low English confidence.

Visual story:

- Hard textbook paragraph.
- Student confused.
- AI explains in simple Marathi-English.
- AI gives key words.
- Student teaches it back.

### AI Helps Understand A Science Diagram

Good for biology, physics, geography, and math diagrams.

Visual story:

- Student sees a diagram.
- AI identifies parts.
- AI explains arrows/labels.
- AI asks "what happens next?"

### AI Creates Practice Questions From A Chapter

Good for exam prep.

Visual story:

- Chapter completed.
- Student does not know how to revise.
- AI creates easy, medium, hard questions.
- Student practices.
- AI gives hints.

### AI Helps Prepare For Oral Exam Or Viva

Good for high school and college.

Visual story:

- Student nervous before viva.
- AI becomes examiner.
- Student answers.
- AI gives feedback.
- Student improves.

## Recommendation

Use `AI Explains A Textbook Page` as the next flagship example.

It is more relatable than customer support for 8th standard students and better represents the project's main promise: people do not know what they can ask AI to do.
