## Story Beats

1. AI learning starts with a mistake.
2. A derivative at one point means the slope of the tangent line: tiny step, tiny change.
3. Derivative becomes a direction clue.
4. AI compares prediction with correct answer and measures error.
5. On the error curve, slope shows which way reduces the mistake.
6. The math glimpse: `slope = Δerror / Δweight`, then `new weight = old weight - small step × slope`.
7. Training is many small corrections, not one big jump.
8. Derivatives matter because they help AI reduce mistakes.
