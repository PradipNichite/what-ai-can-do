## Story Beats

1. Stuck on a textbook page? Show AI the page.
2. Do not ask vaguely. Give AI context.
3. Turn confusion into steps.
4. Do not just copy answers. Practice, get hints, and improve.
5. Best study setup: AI helps, you practice, teacher and textbook verify.
