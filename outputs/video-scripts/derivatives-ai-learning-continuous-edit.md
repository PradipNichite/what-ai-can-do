## Voiceover Script

AI learns a bit like this shot. First it misses. A derivative tells which direction will reduce the mistake. In training, AI compares its prediction with the correct answer, measures the error, and adjusts a little. On the error curve, the slope points toward lower error. The math is simple in spirit: new weight equals old weight minus a small step times the slope. Repeat this many times, and mistakes become smaller. That is why derivatives matter in AI.
