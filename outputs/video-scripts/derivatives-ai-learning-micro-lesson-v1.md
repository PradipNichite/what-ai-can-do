## Voiceover Script

In this micro lesson, you will learn how derivatives help AI reduce mistakes. Why it matters: training AI means reducing error. Think of a missed basketball shot. The miss tells you which way to adjust. In math, a derivative is slope at one point. It tells how a tiny change affects the result. In AI, the model predicts, checks the correct answer, and measures error. The derivative points toward lower error. So AI takes a small step, checks again, and repeats. Remember: derivative tells AI which way to adjust.
