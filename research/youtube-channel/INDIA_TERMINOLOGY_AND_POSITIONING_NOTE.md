# India Terminology And Positioning Note

This note corrects an important India-specific positioning issue.

## Key Insight

In India, many people informally use "school" to mean up to Class 10.

Class 11 and Class 12 are often thought of as:

- junior college
- higher secondary
- intermediate
- plus one / plus two
- college-like study

So if the channel says only "school math," some Indian viewers may assume the channel is mainly for younger students and may not immediately include Class 11-12 topics such as vectors, matrices, calculus, probability, and linear programming.

## Public-Facing Language Rule

Avoid relying only on:

```text
school math
```

Prefer:

```text
Class 9-12 math
```

or:

```text
school and junior college math
```

or:

```text
the math you study in Class 9-12
```

or, for broader branding:

```text
the math you study
```

## Updated Tagline

Previous:

```text
The math you know, inside the AI you use.
```

Better for India:

```text
The math you study, inside the AI you use.
```

Why:

- includes Class 9-12 naturally
- avoids the "school only means till 10th" issue
- still feels simple and memorable
- works for students who do not yet feel they "know" the math

## Updated Channel Promise

Use:

```text
Class 9-12 math explained through real AI mechanisms.
```

or:

```text
School and junior college math explained through AI.
```

For YouTube packaging, the first version is cleaner.

## Recommended Public Description Language

Use:

```text
We connect Class 9-12 math to real AI mechanisms: images as matrices, meaning as vectors, probability as confidence, statistics as pattern learning, and derivatives as improvement.
```

Avoid:

```text
We connect school math to AI.
```

unless the same paragraph also says Class 9-12.

## Playlist Naming

Better playlist names:

- Class 9-12 Math Inside AI
- Why Am I Learning This?
- Math You Study, AI You Use
- AI Mechanisms With Class 9-12 Math

Use "School Math To AI" cautiously. It is clear globally, but in India it may feel Class 10 and below.

Recommended flagship playlist:

```text
Class 9-12 Math Inside AI
```

Alternative, more emotional:

```text
Why Am I Learning This?
```

## Title Guidance

Use exact class references when helpful:

- Class 12 Matrices Are Used In AI Vision
- Class 12 Vectors Help Explain Embeddings
- Class 11 Functions Help Explain AI Models
- Class 10 Probability Helps Explain AI Confidence

This makes the Indian syllabus connection obvious without depending on the word "school."

## Internal Language

Internally, "school math" can still be used as shorthand, but public-facing material should prefer:

```text
Class 9-12 math
```

or:

```text
the math students already study
```

```text
What AI Can Do
```

Tagline:

```text

```

First flagship playlist:

